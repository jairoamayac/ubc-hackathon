## Fixed

| # | Area | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worker | `/api/sponsor` co-signed fee-only transactions (compute budget only, made-up instructions, no compute limit): anyone could burn the sponsor's SOL | Exactly one compute limit ≤ 400k; at least one Kept instruction; only the instructions the app sends; simulated on QuickNode before signing; 120 req/min per IP |
| 2 | Worker / program | Sponsor-paid rent sinks: old tryhard calls (rent recoverable by the attacker), bets | Not sponsored any more; `open_call` and `create_bet` turned off in the program |
| 3 | Worker | `/api/topup` unlimited | 10 per minute per IP and per account |
| 4 | Worker | `/api/rpc` checked the parsed JSON but forwarded the raw text (duplicate keys could smuggle a method) | Forwards the re-serialized request it checked |
| 5 | Worker | `/api/rpc` had no rate limit and relayed any transaction | 600 req/min per IP; at most 2 scans per request; `sendTransaction` only for sponsor-paid transactions |
| 6 | Worker | `kamino_supply` was co-signable with any amount for any pot | Only "whole vault" (amount 0), as the app's wrapper sends it |
| 7 | Worker | Raw exception text echoed to callers | Generic message; details only in logs |
| 8 | Program | After the voting window a single "no" vote could take someone's stake | Nobody loses money without a strict majority of the other participants; otherwise more yes than no keeps it, anything else refunds |
| 9 | Program | Bets could lock stakes forever; tryhard was a free option paid from the house vault (which also holds the fees) | Both turned off (`Disabled`); existing bets can still be voted and claimed |
| 10 | Privy | If Privy took >10 s the app silently switched to a device-only account (money stranded there) | Shows "can't reach sign-in" with Retry and a link to the demo |
| 11 | Privy | A failed account setup left people on "Setting up your account" forever | Error with Try again and Sign out |
| 12 | Privy | No way to sign out | Sign out on Me; data and pending connections reset when the account changes |
| 13 | Privy | A signer that never answers froze every action | 60 s signing timeout |
| 14 | App | "Try again" after a slow confirmation could repeat a money action | Says "Still saving, check your balance"; the screen refreshes after errors too |
| 15 | App | Raw technical errors could reach people | Generic message for code-level failures; "crew" → "group" |
| 16 | Grok UI | Polling could spin forever, raw upstream errors shown | Stops on 4xx or after 10 minutes; plain messages |
| 17 | UX | The live app and the demo looked like two different apps (two welcomes, two logins) | One welcome: Get started (Privy) or Try the demo; the demo opens on Home and links back to sign in |

## Still open (decisions, not quick fixes)

- **Sybil voters (contract H1).** Anyone with an invite link can join a group and a pact; enough fake members can
  still form a majority. Real fix: invite-only joining (creator signs or an invite code) and a voter list fixed when
  the pact ends. Needs a product decision and a program change.
- **Kamino trust (M1/M2).** Pots live in the team's own Kamino market on devnet; its owner could change the reserve.
  If Kamino can't pay out (fully borrowed), money actions wait until it can. Mainnet: use Kamino's main market and
  allow partial redeems.
- **Locking money is two transactions** when free money must move in first; a failure between them leaves the money
  in the group (not lost). Could be one transaction.
- **Grok rate limit** uses KV (not atomic); move to a rate-limit binding and add Turnstile on `/api/appeal`.
- **QuickNode Streams webhook** uses a fixed header secret (no HMAC/timestamp); dormant until `open_dispute` exists.
- **Local demo key** lives in `localStorage` (only when Privy is not configured).
